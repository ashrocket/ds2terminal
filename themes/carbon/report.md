## How to read this report

- APCA Lc and WCAG are measured against the background. The gates are in `colorlab.GATES`.
- "source" names the design-system token, or says `synthesized`, `derived:...`, `alias:...` or `override`.
- A repair changes a color only to pass a hard gate. The report lists each change.
- "Character" rows are targets, not gates. They show how much of the design system the theme keeps.
- "Claude Code" rows show the contrast of the pairs that Claude Code draws, for its ANSI theme and its standard theme.
